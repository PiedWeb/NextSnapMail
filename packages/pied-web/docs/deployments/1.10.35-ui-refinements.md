# Refactoring UI follow-through, 22 October 2026

Pied Web **1.10.35** completes 15 confirmed interface refinements after a full
review against Steve Schoger and Adam Wathan's Refactoring UI. Compatibility:
Nextcloud **35.0.2**, NextSnapMail **0.1.16**, SnappyMail **2.38.2**.

## Delivered behavior

- Phone reader: one 56 px primary toolbar, contextual Back header, copy/reminder
  moved as original bound nodes into More, visible copy feedback outside the menu.
- Metadata: at least 14 px even with Nextcloud's 15 px root; a single sender address
  owns its full row. Long subjects, addresses, conversation headers and reply actions wrap.
- Phone lists: global timestamps share a line with tools without raised boxes;
  native subjects expose their complete title, pagination has names and keyboard
  activation, and empty native search offers its own recovery command.
- Menus fit and scroll within the viewport, received files use one phone column
  with two-line names, settings have a 52rem measure and comfortable touch labels.
- Date panels fit short phone viewports, use 16 px inputs, describe and focus invalid
  fields, expose busy state and recover after refused/rejected scheduling.
- Reduced-motion chrome and unavailable pagination states respect their actual meaning.

Native commands, account boundaries, attachment serialization, received HTML and
editable content remain native. Build output omits implementation comments while
preserving licences and quoted strings; theme transfer is smaller than 1.10.34.
